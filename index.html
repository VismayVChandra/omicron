From low tide to high tide

Three stages, each one gaining on the last.

The gauge below tracks how much of the work Swell has taken on by that stage — you supply less as the tide comes in.

01 — Low tide

You bring the topic

A sentence, a rough outline, or a doc you paste in. Swell reads it for structure, not just keywords.

02 — Rising
