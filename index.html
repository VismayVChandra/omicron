From low tide to high tide

Three stages, each one gaining on the last.

The gauge below tracks how much of the work Swell has taken on by that stage — you supply less as the tide comes in.

01 — Low tide

You bring the topic

A sentence, a rough outline, or a doc you paste in. Swell reads it for structure, not just keywords.

02 — Rising

Swell drafts the whole thing

Copy, layout, imagery, and citations arrive together as editable cards — not a blank canvas with suggestions on the side.

03 — High tide

Nudge tone, swap a section, resize an image. Every change ripples through the rest of the piece automatically.
